2. SERVICE OVERVIEW
BigManage is a chat-prompt-driven collaboration and company-management tool that extracts and organizes information from user chat prompts to manage companies, employees, positions, hierarchy, access controls, attachments, links, reminders, and integrations with third-party messaging platforms. The Service is intentionally conversational — most actions are performed via chat prompts rather than traditional menus and buttons.

3. SUPPORTED PLATFORMS
BigManage can be used via Instagram, Meta Messenger, WhatsApp, Discord, Telegram, email and similar messaging platforms. When you connect BigManage to a third-party platform, you must also comply with that platform’s terms and policies.

4. KEY FEATURES (SUMMARY)
• Company Management: create, join, switch, transfer, and rename companies.
• Employee & Position Management: add employees (by email or accept request), create/rename/delete positions, assign/unassign employees to positions, set and modify hierarchical relations. Note: once an employee joins a company they cannot be removed by deletion—only their access can be managed.  
• Access Controls: general access, time-limited access, past (historical) access and purpose-based access can be set at company, position, or employee level. 
• Managers: managers have elevated permissions (create positions/departments, manage access, and perform other company-wide actions).
• Attachments & Files: create/upload images and non-image files, create images from text or reference images, modify files, search attachments.
• Links & External Resources: add, study and manage external URLs for company/position/employee reference.
• Reminders & Notifications: schedule company-, position-, or employee-level reminders and notifications with flexible recurrence.
• Customization: modify timezone per user/company, automatic language selection based on last-used language, optional voice replies (where supported).

5. ACCOUNT & COMPANY RULES
a. Creating / Joining: To begin you create or request to join a company. Company creators/owners may transfer ownership following verification procedures.
b. Employee Lifecycle: Employees may be added by email or join by request. Employees who join a company cannot be deleted; administrators should instead change access levels if separation is required.
c. Roles & Hierarchy: Positions (roles) may be created, renamed, assigned and deleted; hierarchical relationships can be adjusted to represent reporting lines.
